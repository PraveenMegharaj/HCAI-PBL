Informed consent

Please read the information below before starting the study.

What the study is about

You will help us evaluate two interfaces for eliciting movie preferences. In one you compare two movies at a time; in the other you rank a small set. The study takes about 5–10 minutes and is done in the browser.

What is recorded

  • Your responses (which movie you preferred, or the order you gave to a set)
  • How long each response took
  • The condition you were assigned to
  • Optional demographic categories you provide below

No name, email, IP address, or other personally identifying information is stored.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You can close the browser tab at any time to stop. Data collected up to that point may still be used for analysis.
