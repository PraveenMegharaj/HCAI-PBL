Task 4 · Active learning for expert competence

New setting: no expert labels available. A budget of B expert queries is given. Strategy: least-confident uncertainty sampling — pick the B points where the classifier's top-class probability is smallest and query the expert there. From those queries we estimate the expert's accuracy conditioned on the model's predicted class, then defer whenever that estimated accuracy exceeds the model's confidence.

Those uncertain points are exactly where deferral might help — labels there give the most information about whether the expert should be trusted.
